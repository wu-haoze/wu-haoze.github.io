heading: "New course in Spring 2027"
description: "As generative AI makes it possible to interact with computers through everyday language, it opens up new ways of approaching problems, developing ideas, and creating things. Using generative AI effectively depends on how well we formulate problems, articulate what we want, evaluate results, and make adjustments. In this course, students will learn how to turn vague goals into clear instructions, break complex tasks into manageable parts, evaluate what AI produces, and improve results through testing and revision. We will also explore how chatbots and AI assistants do what they do, why they sometimes succeed or fail, and how they can be used thoughtfully in writing, research, creative work, planning, and everyday problem-solving. In addition, we will discuss the societal implications and ethical considerations that arise from their use. No technical background is expected or required."
